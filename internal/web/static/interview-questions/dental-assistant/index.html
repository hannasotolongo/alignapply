Dental Assistant Interview Questions and Answers

Dental assistant interviews cover chairside skills, infection control and keeping patients comfortable, along with how you work with the dentist. Here are the 10 questions you're most likely to hear, and how to answer each one.

Tip: for any “tell me about a time” question, use the STAR method.

1. Tell me about yourself.

What they want to know: A quick, relevant summary that shows why you fit this role.

How to answer: Use present, past, future: what you do now, one or two wins that match this job, and why this role is your next step. Keep it under two minutes.

2. Why do you want to work here?

What they want to know: That you picked them on purpose, not at random.

How to answer: Name one specific thing about the company (its patients, customers, product or reputation) and connect it to what you enjoy doing.

3. What chairside procedures are you comfortable assisting with?

What they want to know: Day-one skills.

How to answer: List what you have done (four-handed dentistry, fillings, crowns, extractions, impressions) and any certifications like radiography.

4. How do you follow infection control protocols?

What they want to know: Patient safety.

How to answer: Walk through sterilizing instruments, disinfecting operatories, PPE and following OSHA and CDC guidance.

5. How do you help a nervous patient feel comfortable?

What they want to know: Patient care.

How to answer: Describe explaining steps, checking in during the procedure, and a real example of calming someone.

6. Which dental software have you used?

What they want to know: Ramp-up time.

How to answer: Mention Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental or others, and charting or scheduling tasks you did.

7. How do you anticipate the dentist's needs?

What they want to know: Teamwork.

How to answer: Explain preparing trays ahead, following the procedure step by step, and an example of staying one step ahead.

8. How do you handle a busy day with back-to-back patients?

What they want to know: Efficiency.

How to answer: Talk about turning rooms over quickly, staying organized and keeping quality high.

9. Have you taken dental X-rays?

What they want to know: Credentials.

How to answer: Be clear about your radiography certification and experience with digital or film X-rays.

10. Why do you want to work at this practice?

What they want to know: Fit.

How to answer: Mention something specific: their patient focus, technology, or reputation in the community.

How to answer the classics

  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why do you want to work here
  • What is your greatest weakness
  • What is your greatest strength
  • Why should we hire you
