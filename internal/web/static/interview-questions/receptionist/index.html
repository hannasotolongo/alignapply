Receptionist Interview Questions and Answers

Receptionist interviews look for a friendly, organized person who can manage the front desk, phones and visitors at the same time. Here are the 10 questions you're most likely to hear, and how to answer each one.

Tip: for any “tell me about a time” question, use the STAR method.

1. Tell me about yourself.

What they want to know: A quick, relevant summary that shows why you fit this role.

How to answer: Use present, past, future: what you do now, one or two wins that match this job, and why this role is your next step. Keep it under two minutes.

2. Why do you want to work here?

What they want to know: That you picked them on purpose, not at random.

How to answer: Name one specific thing about the company (its patients, customers, product or reputation) and connect it to what you enjoy doing.

3. How would you greet a visitor while the phone is ringing?

What they want to know: Multitasking with good manners.

How to answer: Acknowledge the visitor with a smile and a quick 'I'll be right with you,' answer the call politely, then give the visitor your full attention.

4. How do you handle a rude caller?

What they want to know: Patience.

How to answer: Stay calm, keep your tone friendly, focus on what they need, and transfer or take a message when that is the right step.

5. What phone systems or scheduling software have you used?

What they want to know: Ramp-up time.

How to answer: List what you know and say you pick up new systems quickly, with an example.

6. How do you stay organized at a busy front desk?

What they want to know: Your system.

How to answer: Mention keeping a log of calls and visitors, a tidy desk, and checklists for opening and closing.

7. Tell me about a time you made a great first impression on someone.

What they want to know: That you understand you are the face of the company.

How to answer: Share a short story where your welcome or help made a visitor or customer happy.

8. How would you handle confidential information you overhear?

What they want to know: Discretion.

How to answer: Say you keep it private, never repeat it, and follow company policy.

9. What would you do if two people arrived at once and both were in a hurry?

What they want to know: Calm prioritizing.

How to answer: Greet both, quickly find out what each needs, handle the fastest request first, and keep both updated.

10. Why do you want to be a receptionist here?

What they want to know: Fit and motivation.

How to answer: Connect what you enjoy (helping people, keeping things running smoothly) to this specific office.

How to answer the classics

  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why do you want to work here
  • What is your greatest weakness
  • What is your greatest strength
  • Why should we hire you
