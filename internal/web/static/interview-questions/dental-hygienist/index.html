Dental Hygienist Interview Questions and Answers

Dental hygienist interviews focus on clinical skill, patient education, time management and how you fit with the doctor and team. Here are the 10 questions you're most likely to hear, and how to answer each one.

Tip: for any “tell me about a time” question, use the STAR method.

1. Tell me about yourself.

What they want to know: A quick, relevant summary that shows why you fit this role.

How to answer: Use present, past, future: what you do now, one or two wins that match this job, and why this role is your next step. Keep it under two minutes.

2. Why do you want to work here?

What they want to know: That you picked them on purpose, not at random.

How to answer: Name one specific thing about the company (its patients, customers, product or reputation) and connect it to what you enjoy doing.

3. How do you educate patients about their oral health?

What they want to know: Communication that changes habits.

How to answer: Explain how you keep it simple and positive, tailor advice to the patient, and an example of a patient who improved.

4. How do you handle a patient who refuses X-rays or treatment?

What they want to know: Respect plus persuasion.

How to answer: Say you explain the why calmly, document the refusal, and let the dentist know.

5. How do you stay on schedule with full hygiene days?

What they want to know: Time management.

How to answer: Describe your routine for each appointment and how you handle a patient who needs more time.

6. How do you detect and communicate periodontal disease?

What they want to know: Clinical judgment.

How to answer: Mention probing, charting, explaining results to the patient and recommending next steps with the dentist.

7. What is your experience with scaling and root planing?

What they want to know: Skills.

How to answer: Be specific about volume, techniques and any local anesthesia certification.

8. How do you make anxious patients comfortable?

What they want to know: Patient care.

How to answer: Share your approach and a real example.

9. How do you support the practice's treatment acceptance?

What they want to know: Teamwork and business sense.

How to answer: Explain how you identify needs, educate the patient, and hand off clearly to the dentist.

10. Why this practice?

What they want to know: Fit.

How to answer: Mention their philosophy, technology, patients or team culture.

  • Why do you want to work here
  • What is your greatest weakness
  • What is your greatest strength
  • Why should we hire you
