Bank Teller Interview Questions and Answers

Bank teller interviews focus on accuracy with cash, customer service, and following security and compliance rules. Here are the 10 questions you're most likely to hear, and how to answer each one.

Tip: for any “tell me about a time” question, use the STAR method.

1. Tell me about yourself.

What they want to know: A quick, relevant summary that shows why you fit this role.

How to answer: Use present, past, future: what you do now, one or two wins that match this job, and why this role is your next step. Keep it under two minutes.

2. Why do you want to work here?

What they want to know: That you picked them on purpose, not at random.

How to answer: Name one specific thing about the company (its patients, customers, product or reputation) and connect it to what you enjoy doing.

3. How do you make sure your cash drawer balances?

What they want to know: Accuracy.

How to answer: Explain counting carefully, double-checking large transactions, and what you do if you are off.

4. What would you do if a customer became upset about a fee?

What they want to know: Service under pressure.

How to answer: Listen, explain the fee clearly, check what options exist, and escalate to a manager if appropriate.

5. How would you handle a transaction that seems suspicious?

What they want to know: Fraud awareness and compliance.

How to answer: Say you would follow bank procedures, stay calm with the customer, and notify a supervisor.

6. Tell me about a time you recommended a product to a customer.

What they want to know: Comfort with referrals.

How to answer: Share how you noticed a need and connected the customer with the right product or banker.

7. How do you handle a long line of customers?

What they want to know: Speed with accuracy.

How to answer: Talk about staying focused, being friendly but efficient, and never skipping verification steps.

8. Why is following procedures important at a bank?

What they want to know: Understanding of risk.

How to answer: Mention protecting customers' money and identity, and the bank's trust.

9. Do you have cash handling experience?

What they want to know: Relevant background.

How to answer: Share retail, restaurant or other roles where you handled cash, with your accuracy record.

10. Why do you want to work at this bank?

What they want to know: Fit.

How to answer: Mention something about their community involvement, customers or values.

  • Why do you want to work here
  • What is your greatest weakness
  • What is your greatest strength
  • Why should we hire you
