Customer Service Representative Interview Questions and Answers

Customer service interviews are about patience, problem-solving and staying positive with frustrated customers, while still following company policy. Here are the 10 questions you're most likely to hear, and how to answer each one.

Tip: for any “tell me about a time” question, use the STAR method.

1. Tell me about yourself.

What they want to know: A quick, relevant summary that shows why you fit this role.

How to answer: Use present, past, future: what you do now, one or two wins that match this job, and why this role is your next step. Keep it under two minutes.

2. Why do you want to work here?

What they want to know: That you picked them on purpose, not at random.

How to answer: Name one specific thing about the company (its patients, customers, product or reputation) and connect it to what you enjoy doing.

3. What does good customer service mean to you?

What they want to know: Whether your idea of service matches theirs.

How to answer: Give a simple definition, like solving the problem the first time while making the customer feel heard, and an example of you doing it.

4. Tell me about a time you dealt with an angry customer.

What they want to know: How you stay calm and fix things.

How to answer: Use STAR: what upset them, how you listened and apologized for the experience, what you did to solve it, and how it ended.

5. How do you handle a request you can't say yes to?

What they want to know: Balancing policy with keeping the customer happy.

How to answer: Explain that you say what you can do instead, offer alternatives, and escalate when appropriate, with an example.

6. How do you handle a high volume of calls or chats?

What they want to know: Speed without losing quality.

How to answer: Mention staying organized, using notes or templates, and keeping a positive tone even on call number fifty.

7. Describe a time you went above and beyond for a customer.

What they want to know: Initiative and care.

How to answer: Share a specific story with a clear result, like a saved order, a returning customer, or great feedback.

8. How do you stay positive during a tough shift?

What they want to know: Resilience.

How to answer: Mention a short reset after hard calls, not taking it personally, and focusing on the next customer.

9. What customer service tools have you used?

What they want to know: Ramp-up time.

How to answer: Name what you know (Zendesk, Salesforce, Freshdesk, phone systems, live chat) and say you learn new systems quickly.

10. Why should we hire you?

What they want to know: Your best fit in one answer.

How to answer: Pick two or three strengths that match the job post and back each with a quick example or result.

How to answer the classics

  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why do you want to work here
  • What is your greatest weakness
  • What is your greatest strength
  • Why should we hire you
