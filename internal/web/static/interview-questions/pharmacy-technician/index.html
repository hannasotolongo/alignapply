Pharmacy Technician Interview Questions and Answers

Pharmacy technician interviews focus on accuracy, patient privacy, insurance and staying calm when the pharmacy is busy. Here are the 10 questions you're most likely to hear, and how to answer each one.

Tip: for any “tell me about a time” question, use the STAR method.

1. Tell me about yourself.

What they want to know: A quick, relevant summary that shows why you fit this role.

How to answer: Use present, past, future: what you do now, one or two wins that match this job, and why this role is your next step. Keep it under two minutes.

2. Why do you want to work here?

What they want to know: That you picked them on purpose, not at random.

How to answer: Name one specific thing about the company (its patients, customers, product or reputation) and connect it to what you enjoy doing.

3. How do you make sure prescriptions are filled accurately?

What they want to know: Patient safety.

How to answer: Describe checking the drug, strength, quantity and patient details, and following the pharmacist's verification process.

4. How would you handle an upset customer whose prescription isn't ready?

What they want to know: Service under pressure.

How to answer: Apologize, explain the reason clearly, give a realistic time, and see what can be done to help.

5. What experience do you have with insurance claims?

What they want to know: Practical skills.

How to answer: Mention handling rejections, prior authorizations, and calling insurers or prescribers.

6. How do you protect patient privacy?

What they want to know: HIPAA knowledge.

How to answer: Verify identity, keep voices low at the counter, and only share information with the right people.

7. What would you do if you noticed a possible error on a prescription?

What they want to know: Speaking up.

How to answer: Stop and tell the pharmacist right away; never guess.

8. How do you handle a busy rush?

What they want to know: Efficiency.

How to answer: Stay organized, focus on one prescription at a time, and communicate with the team.

9. Are you certified?

What they want to know: Credentials.

How to answer: Mention PTCB or ExCPT certification and your state registration.

10. Why do you want to work in this pharmacy?

What they want to know: Fit.

How to answer: Connect retail, hospital or specialty pharmacy to what you enjoy.

How to answer the classics

  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why do you want to work here
  • What is your greatest weakness
  • What is your greatest strength
  • Why should we hire you
