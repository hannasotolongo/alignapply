Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Jane Careers collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to run the Service, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, your name if you provide it, and your password, which we store only as a secure one-way hash.
  • Résumés: when you upload a PDF, it is read in your browser. We store the text of the résumé and its file name. We do not store the PDF file itself.
  • Career profile and preferences: the headline, summary, experience, skills, education, certifications, licenses, and projects you enter or confirm, plus your target role, location, salary, and work-style preferences.
  • Job search and applications: searches you run, jobs you save, and the status history of applications you track.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your Stripe customer ID, subscription status, and plan. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Gmail (only if you connect it): with your permission, we use read-only access to recent messages to detect job-application updates, such as confirmations, interview invitations, and rejections, and update your tracked jobs. See "Google user data" below.
  • Usage and technical data: a count of searches (to apply plan limits), and standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and request times, used for security and troubleshooting.

How we use information

  • To provide the Service: matching your experience to job requirements, recommending résumés, and tracking applications.
  • To process payments and manage your plan.
  • To secure the Service, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account, billing, or important changes.

We do not use your résumé or profile for advertising, and we do not share it with employers unless you choose to apply to them yourself.

Service providers we share data with

We share information only with providers that help us run the Service, and only as needed:

  • OpenAI processes résumé, profile, and job-posting text to compare your experience with job requirements. It is used only to produce results for you.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Render hosts our servers and database.
  • Google provides Gmail access if you connect it, and serves the website's fonts.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the Service.

Google user data

If you connect Gmail, Jane Careers requests read-only access (gmail.readonly). We use it only to find emails related to your job applications and to update the status of jobs you are tracking in Jane Careers. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not allow humans to read it except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, or as required by law. Jane Careers's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect Gmail at any time from My jobs, or from your Google Account settings.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete individual résumés at any time, and you can delete your entire account from Plan & billing. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, résumés, preferences, tracked jobs, and Gmail connection, and cancels any active subscription. Limited billing records may be kept as required for tax and accounting purposes.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls that keep each user's data private to that user. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.
