Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

We want CareerStore to be worth what you pay. If it isn't, here's how cancellations and refunds work.

Free trial

Pro Monthly starts with a 3-day free trial. You'll need a card to start it, but you won't be charged if you cancel before the trial ends: Plan & billing → Manage or cancel trial. If you don't cancel, your subscription starts and the first monthly payment is charged when the trial ends.

Cancelling a subscription

  • Cancel any time in the app: Plan & billing → Manage billing → Cancel.
  • Your Pro access continues until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.
  • We don't charge cancellation fees.

Refunds

  • First purchase, 7-day guarantee: if you're not satisfied with your first payment for any plan, email us within 7 days of purchase and we'll refund it in full.
  • Renewals: subscription renewals aren't refunded for partial periods. If you forgot to cancel and haven't used Pro features since the renewal, contact us within 7 days and we'll refund that renewal.
  • Service problems: if a technical problem on our side prevented you from using features you paid for, contact us and we'll make it right with a refund or credit.
  • School and program licenses: refundable in full within 14 days of the first purchase if your students haven't started using CareerStore. After that, licenses aren't refunded for partial years, but you can cancel renewal at any time.
  • We may decline refund requests that show abuse, such as repeated purchase-and-refund cycles.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe, usually within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Contact
