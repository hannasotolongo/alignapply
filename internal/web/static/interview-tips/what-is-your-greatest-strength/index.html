How to Answer “What Is Your Greatest Strength?”

Pick one strength that matters for this job and prove it with a short story.

Why interviewers ask it

They want to know what you will be best at on their team.

A simple way to structure your answer

  1. Choose one strength: Match it to the job description.
  2. Prove it: A specific example with a result.
  3. Connect it: How it will help in this role.

Example answer

My biggest strength is staying calm with upset customers. At my last job I handled our escalated calls, and I kept a 95% satisfaction score on them. In this role, where you handle a lot of billing questions, I think that would help right away.

Make it yours: swap in your own job, numbers and goals.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Listing five strengths with no proof
  • Choosing something that doesn't matter for the job
  • Being too modest to give a real example

Practice your interview out loud

Reading answers isn't the same as saying them. Pick your interview stage, answer Jane's questions out loud, and get feedback on your answer, pace and filler words. Then try again.
