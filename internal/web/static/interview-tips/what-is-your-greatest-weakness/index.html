How to Answer “What Is Your Greatest Weakness?”

This question is about self-awareness, not a trap. The best answers are honest, small and show real progress.

Why interviewers ask it

Interviewers want to see that you know yourself and are working to improve.

A simple way to structure your answer

  1. Name a real weakness: Something genuine that is not central to the job.
  2. Show the impact: Briefly, how it used to affect your work.
  3. Show the fix: What you do now, and the improvement.

Example answer

I used to say yes to every request, which meant I sometimes stretched myself too thin. Now I check my priorities before taking something on and I'm upfront about timelines. My manager told me in my last review that my deadlines got much more reliable.

Make it yours: swap in your own job, numbers and goals.

Mistakes to avoid

  • “I'm a perfectionist” with no real detail
  • Saying you have no weaknesses
  • Naming a weakness that is a core skill for the job
