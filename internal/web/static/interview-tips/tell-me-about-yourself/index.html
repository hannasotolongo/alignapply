How to Answer “Tell Me About Yourself”

It is usually the first question, and it sets the tone for the whole interview. The goal is not your life story. It is a short pitch for why you fit this job.

Why interviewers ask it

The interviewer wants a quick, relevant overview, and to hear how you communicate. A focused answer makes every later question easier.

A simple way to structure your answer

  1. Present: What you do now, and one thing you are good at.
  2. Past: One or two experiences or wins that match this job.
  3. Future: Why this role is the right next step for you.

Example answer

I'm an administrative assistant at a busy dental office, where I manage the schedule for four providers and handle billing questions. Last year I set up a reminder system that cut no-shows by about a third. I'm looking for an office manager role where I can take on more of the operations side, which is why this position caught my eye.

Make it yours: swap in your own job, numbers and goals.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Reciting your whole résumé in order
  • Going past two minutes
  • Sharing personal details that have nothing to do with the job
  • Not ending with why you want this role

Practice your interview out loud

Reading answers isn't the same as saying them. Pick your interview stage, answer Jane's questions out loud, and get feedback on your answer, pace and filler words. Then try again.
