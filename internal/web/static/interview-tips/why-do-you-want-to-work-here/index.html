How to Answer “Why Do You Want to Work Here?”

This question checks whether you chose this company on purpose. A generic answer sounds like you are applying everywhere.

Why interviewers ask it

Employers want people who will stay and care about the work. Showing you researched them signals both.

A simple way to structure your answer

  1. Something specific about them: Their customers, mission, reputation, product or growth.
  2. Why it matters to you: Connect it to what you enjoy or value.
  3. What you would bring: One strength that helps them with it.

Example answer

I've followed how your clinic expanded into community health days, and that focus on patients who don't usually get care is exactly why I went into healthcare. With my bilingual experience rooming patients, I think I could help make those visits smoother.

Make it yours: swap in your own job, numbers and goals.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Talking only about pay or benefits
  • Saying something that fits any company
  • Not knowing what the company does

Practice your interview out loud

Reading answers isn't the same as saying them. Pick your interview stage, answer Jane's questions out loud, and get feedback on your answer, pace and filler words. Then try again.
