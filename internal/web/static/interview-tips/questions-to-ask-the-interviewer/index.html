Good Questions to Ask the Interviewer

“Do you have any questions for us?” is part of the interview. Asking good questions shows real interest and helps you decide if the job is right for you.

Why interviewers ask it

Interviewers notice candidates who are curious about the work, the team and success in the role.

A simple way to structure your answer

  1. About the role: What does a typical day look like? What would success look like in the first 90 days?
  2. About the team: Who would I work with most? How does the team communicate?
  3. About growth: How do people usually grow in this role?
  4. About next steps: What are the next steps in the process?

Example answer

What would you want the person in this role to have accomplished in the first three months?

Make it yours: swap in your own job, numbers and goals.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Saying you have no questions
  • Asking only about pay and time off in the first interview
  • Asking something answered on their website

Practice your interview out loud

Reading answers isn't the same as saying them. Pick your interview stage, answer Jane's questions out loud, and get feedback on your answer, pace and filler words. Then try again.
